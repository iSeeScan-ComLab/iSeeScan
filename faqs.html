💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Both standards determine the pinout arrangement of individual wires inside an RJ45 connector. T568B is the most widely used standard in modern commercial network installations, while T568A is commonly found in residential or older legacy networks. As long as both ends of the cable use the exact same standard, performance is identical.

A Straight-Through cable connects different types of network hardware (e.g., Computer to Switch, or Switch to Router). A Crossover cable connects identical devices directly together (e.g., Computer to Computer). Note that most modern switch ports feature Auto-MDIX and automatically adapt to either cable configuration.

Connect both ends of your terminated cable into a standard Network Cable Tester. Power on the device and watch the pin LEDs light up. If all 8 pins light up sequentially in the exact same numerical order (1 through 8) on both tester units, your pinout has full continuity and is properly wired.

To prepare and crimp a custom patch cable, you will need Cat5e or Cat6 bulk cable, RJ45 modular connectors, a wire stripper/cutter tool, an RJ45 crimping tool, and a digital network cable tester for final validation.
