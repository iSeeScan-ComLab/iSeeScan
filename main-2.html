What is a Motherboard?

The motherboard is the main printed circuit board (PCB) inside a computer. It serves as the central hub that connects and allows communication between the CPU, RAM, storage drives, graphics cards, and all other physical internal or external peripherals.

Click a highlighted motherboard zone to view its details and see what components connect there.
